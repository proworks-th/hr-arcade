# Verify baseline, 15 Nov 2026 (PRD phone-first, Phase 0)

Simulated numbers from `python3 verify/run.py` (headless Chromium, SwiftShader, test mode `?test=1` on the mocks).
Frame rate: 5 s of `requestAnimationFrame` right after the game starts, CPU slowed 4×. Cells read
`average fps / worst frame ms`. Warn under 55 fps. These are a guide only; the real numbers are the owner's
`?fps=1` screenshots from the iPhone 11 (VERIFY.md, "Measuring on the real iPhone"), still to come.

Phone warnings: findings from `verify/phone-checks.js` per screen (all shots of the screen added up), on
`iphone11` (414×896) and `narrow` (390×844). They are warnings until `PHONE_STRICT = True`.

Pre-existing failures (also on live main, not fixed here): village pet coat buttons covered by the map (desktop),
farm basket button overlap (narrow, `farm-bag`).
